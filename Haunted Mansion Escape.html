Teacher corner: answer key and how to run it

Students work through five rooms in order. Rooms 1–4 each award one digit of the crypt code (1, 0, 3, 1 — it spells Halloween, 10/31). Room 5 has a final system question and then the keypad. Every wrong answer shows students how to verify their point by substituting it into both equations, which is the heart of TEKS 8.9A: identify and verify the values of x and y that simultaneously satisfy two linear equations in the form y = mx + b from the intersections of the graphed equations.

The escape screen shows time, wrong tries and hints used, plus a spot for the student's name, so a screenshot works as a ticket out. The game resets when the page is reloaded or "Play again" is pressed.
